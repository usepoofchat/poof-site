#Overview

The Poof bot runs in the official Poof groups. It does three things.

01Commands/links, /docs, /ca, /rules and more. One tap, the official answer. 02FiltersAdmins add their own commands with a text or a photo. The menu updates itself. 03Buy botEvery buy of a token, live, within seconds. The bigger the buy, the bigger the alert.

#Filters ADMINS

A filter is a command that answers with a saved message. Admins create them from the chat, no setup needed. After that, anyone can use them.

CommandWhat it does
Adds a filter, or updates it if it exists. Then /name answers with the text. Bold, italics and links are kept.
reply + /addfilter name
Reply to a message (text, or a photo with a caption) with /addfilter name to save that message as the filter.
Removes a filter. Works for the built-in commands too: /delfilter rules turns /rules off, and /addfilter rules ... brings it back with your text.
Lists custom filters, built-in commands and the ones turned off.

Member/roadmap12:12

  • Names use letters, numbers and _ (up to 32). /Roadmap and /roadmap are the same filter.
  • New filters appear in the / menu and in /help on their own.
  • Some names are reserved and can't be filters: help, start, buybot and the filter commands themselves.

Poof filters use /addfilter, /delfilter and /listfilters. Don't use /filter: other bots answer it too and the group gets double replies.

#Buy bot: set up

The buy bot posts every buy of a token into the group, live. It works on Robinhood Chain with Uniswap v2, v3 and v4 pools, and with pons launches from their bonding curve to Uniswap v4. All /buybot commands are for admins.

  1. Point it at the tokenSend /buybot set 0x... with the token contract address. The bot finds the token's Uniswap pools on its own (up to 3 pools with at least $500 liquidity).
  2. Check the statusThe bot answers with the token, the pools it watches and the current settings. Send /buybot any time to see them again.
  3. Post a test/buybot test posts three sample alerts (Spark, Poof, Big poof) marked as test, so you see the look before real buys come in.
  4. Tune itSet the smallest buy shown, the tiers and how long small alerts stay. See Settings.

EXAMPLE
/buybot set 0x…13:00

Brand new token not found yet? Pools show up once DexScreener lists them. You can also give the pool address (for a v4 pool, its pool id): /buybot set <token> <pair>.

  1. TierThe animation and the name show the size of the buy: Spark, Puff, Poof or Big poof.
  2. Emoji rowGrows with the buy. A Big poof starts at 30 emoji, the row stops at 60. Each tier has its own emoji.
  3. PaidWhat the buyer paid, in ETH and in dollars.
  4. ReceivedTokens bought, with a link to the transaction on the explorer.
  5. BuyerThe wallet, and New holder or Position +x%: how much this buy added to the bag they already had.
  6. MCapMarket cap right after the buy.
  7. Poof timerSmall alerts delete themselves after 10 min (you can change it). Big poofs stay.

Buttons under each alert: Chart opens DexScreener, Buy opens Uniswap with the token ready, usepoof.chat opens the website.

#Tiers

Four sizes, each with its own animation and emoji. By default the limits follow the market cap, so a Big poof stays special as the token grows.

SparkBelow Puff
launch: under $100

at least $100
PoofFrom 0.025% of MCap
at least $500
Big poofFrom 0.1% of MCap
at least $1,500

How auto tiers work

Each limit is a share of the market cap, but never lower than its floor. At $1M MCap the floors apply: Puff from $100, Poof from $500, Big poof from $1,500. At $20M MCap: Puff from $1,000, Poof from $5,000, Big poof from $20,000.

Fixed tiers

Prefer your own numbers? /buybot tiers 100 500 1500 sets where Puff, Poof and Big poof start, in dollars. /buybot tiers auto goes back to automatic.

#Live and grouped

The bot checks the chain about every 3 seconds, so an alert lands a few seconds after the buy.

Telegram lets a bot send about 20 messages per minute in a group. When several buys land in the same few seconds, the biggest one gets the full alert and the others are listed under it, each with its amount and a link to its transaction. No buy is skipped and the chat stays readable, even in a busy minute.

#Questions

No alerts are showing up

Send /buybot and check:

  • It says on. If not, /buybot on.
  • Min buy isn't higher than the buys coming in.
  • The pool is Uniswap v2, v3 or v4 on Robinhood Chain.
  • The pool has at least $500 liquidity and is listed on DexScreener. For a very new pool, use /buybot set <token> <pair>.
Does it show sells?

No. Only buys: a swap where someone pays ETH (or the pool's other token) and receives the tracked token.

Why are some buys listed under another alert?

They landed in the same few seconds. Telegram limits how many messages a bot can send per minute, so the biggest buy gets the full alert and the rest are listed under it. See Live and grouped.

Where does the price come from?

The dollar value is what the buyer paid, at the current ETH price. Market cap and token price come from DexScreener. If no price is available, the bot waits and posts the buy as soon as it has one.

The emoji look plain on my phone

Poof animated emoji show in the Telegram apps. If an admin turns them off with /buybot emoji off, the alert uses plain emoji instead.

Our group became a supergroup. Do we set it up again?

No. The bot follows the group to its new id with every setting.

Which chains are supported?

Robinhood Chain today, with Uniswap v2, v3 and v4 pools and pons launches. More chains can come later.

Does it work with pons launches?

Yes. Send /buybot set with the token address while it is still on its bonding curve. Every curve buy gets an alert, the MCap line says bonding curve, and Chart and Buy open the token on ponsfamily.com. When the curve fills and the token graduates, the bot posts a graduation alert and keeps going on the new Uniswap v4 pool by itself.

Will the bot ever DM me?

No. The bot never sends the first message, never asks for a seed phrase, a private key or wallet access, and never asks you to connect a wallet. Anyone who does is a scammer. Admins never DM first either.
