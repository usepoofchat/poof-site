Security that looks simple.

Behind Poof is a modern cryptographic architecture designed around a simple goal:

Keep the conversation between the people having it.


        
End-to-end encryption

        
Your messages are encrypted between participants rather than being treated as readable server-side content.

      

      

        

        
Post-quantum cryptography

        
Poof incorporates modern post-quantum cryptographic mechanisms designed to address threats from future quantum-capable attackers.

      

      

        

        
Zero-knowledge principles

        
Poof is designed to minimize what the service needs to know about its users and their conversations.

      

      

        

        
Ephemeral by design

        
Rooms have defined lifetimes rather than becoming permanent communication spaces.
